Project intentions

Vision and mission
Restore the River Ock catchment to good ecological condition, reducing nutrient pollution and flood risk while creating habitat and a long term income for local landowners through nature markets.
Key interventions
Riparian buffer planting, two wetland creation areas, leaky woody dams, hedgerow restoration, and reduced fertiliser inputs on enrolled parcels.
Target outcomes
Phosphate and nitrate reduction, natural flood management, biodiversity uplift, and woodland and soil carbon.
