1. Project context

Evenlode covers 187.8 hectares across 18 parcels, 15 of them in hand and 3 let on farm business tenancies expiring in 2027. Seven sites are already mapped, nine projects sit against them.

An SFI agreement runs to 2027 and constrains what can be stacked on the improved grassland, which rules out two of the candidate services before they are scored.

2. Demand

Local demand is strongest for biodiversity net gain. Fourteen major applications sit inside the catchment, of which nine name off site units in their planning statements.

  • GPAP register, 14 major applications, 9 naming off site units
  • Two water companies hold abstraction licences expiring in 2028
  • No public funding route currently open for soil carbon here

The beneficiary map is being drawn now, and the qualified buyer list follows from it. Trade has not yet sense-checked either.

Soil carbon is parked until the peat depth survey lands from the contractor. Nutrient neutrality depends on the catchment permitting position, which the Environment Agency has not confirmed for the upper reaches.

4. Summary

Nothing written yet. The verdict is assembled from the three chapters above once Trade has signed off the buyer list.
