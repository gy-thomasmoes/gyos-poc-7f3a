1 · About static
Companies offset unavoidable emissions by buying credits; one credit equals one tonne of CO2 sequestered or avoided. The most investable and stable of the natural capital markets, split into compliance markets (UK and EU ETS) and the voluntary carbon market.
2 · How we sell it static
Sell verified credits under the Woodland Carbon Code, Peatland Carbon Code, Wilder Carbon or international codes. Buyers currently prefer removals credits over avoidance.
3 · Market readiness 9 categories · GY methodology
Hover a category for its definition. Scored ● active · ● emerging · ● nascent, per the GY Market Readiness Assessment (triangulating registry hard data, intermediary intelligence and GY deal-pipeline insight).

5 · Market dynamics dynamic · refresh ~3 months
•Global VCM ~£6.43B (2024). Retirements up 9% in H1 2025; issuances down 15% as developers wait for buyers.
•Buyers pay premiums for high-quality credits with co-benefits; ICVCM-compliant credits command higher prices and broader access.
•UK high-quality credits sell at £75-125/tCO2; WCC typically £25-50 below Wilder Carbon.
•CDR offtakes surged ~200% year on year to ~20Mt by June 2025.
6 · UK signals dynamic
•~2,500 live domestic projects under WCC / PCC / Wilder Carbon vs only 9 on international registries.
•WCC: 11.34M credits issued; estimated market £396.9M, rising to ~£487.5M by end 2025.
•Domestic buyers: law firms, SMEs, large corporates.
7 · Market risks
•International buyers may not pay UK premium prices; comparable credits are cheaper in the Global South.
•Lingering reputational caution from the 2022-23 critiques.
